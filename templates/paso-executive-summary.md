# PASO Executive Summary

A one-page structure for executive decisions: **Problem · Approach · Solution · Outcome**.

## Problem
*What is broken or at risk, and what does it cost the business today?* (2–3 sentences, and quantify it where you can)

## Approach
*How we will tackle it: phases, principles, who is involved.* (3–5 bullets)

## Solution
*What we will build, buy or change.* Use one diagram or table, not ten.

## Outcome
*What success looks like and how we will measure it.*
