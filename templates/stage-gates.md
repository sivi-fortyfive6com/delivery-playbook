# Stage Gates

| Gate | Name | Key question | Entry evidence | Decision owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G0 | Idea | Is this worth exploring? | Problem statement, sponsor named | Portfolio board |
| G1 | Discovery complete | Do we understand the problem and options? | Current state, options analysis, rough cost | Sponsor |
| G2 | Business case | Should we invest? | Business case, benefits, risks, MoSCoW scope | Steering committee |
| G3 | Ready to build | Are we set up to succeed? | Plan, team, environments, RACI, definition of done | Programme lead |
| G4 | Ready to launch | Is it safe to go live? | Test results, training, support model, rollback plan | Steering committee |
| G5 | Benefits review | Did we get what we paid for? | Benefit metrics vs. baseline, lessons learned | Sponsor |

## Gate review checklist

- [ ] Evidence submitted 3 working days before the gate
- [ ] Risks and issues log updated
- [ ] Budget: actual vs. forecast
- [ ] Decision recorded: **Go / Go with conditions / Hold / Stop**
- [ ] Conditions have owners and dates
