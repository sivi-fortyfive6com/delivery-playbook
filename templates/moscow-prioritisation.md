# MoSCoW Prioritisation

| Category | Meaning | Rule of thumb |
|---|---|---|
| **Must have** | Without it, the release fails or is unsafe or non-compliant | ≤ 60% of effort |
| **Should have** | Important, but there's a workaround | ~20% |
| **Could have** | Nice to have; first to drop | ~20% |
| **Won't have (this time)** | Agreed out of scope for now | Recorded, not forgotten |
